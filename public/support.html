Support

Help with WorkoutsMCP — the website, the MCP server and the iPhone app.

Contact

Email tejas@gja.in with anything: a question, a bug, a workout that did not reach your watch. It is a one-person side project, so expect an answer within a few days. Bugs in the code can also go to the issue tracker.

Common questions

  • A workout is not on my Apple Watch. Open the app: it syncs the plan to Apple Fitness straight away, and the Planned tab says when it last did. On the watch, open the Workout app and scroll up to find WorkoutsMCP. Scheduling must be allowed in Settings › Fitness on the iPhone.
  • Planning with Claude or ChatGPT. In the app, Settings › Connect to Claude walks through adding https://workouts-mcp.com/mcp as a connector and signing in with the same account.
  • Connecting intervals.icu. Sign in on workouts-mcp.com and connect it from the Setup section; planned workouts are pushed there and completed sessions come back.
  • Deleting your account. In the app, Settings › Delete account deletes everything held for you. You can also ask by email.

What is stored and why is in the privacy policy.
