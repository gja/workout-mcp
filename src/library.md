# Triathlon Workout Library
## Common Swim, Bike, and Run Workout Structures
 
This document provides a reference library of common workout types and structures for swimming, cycling, and running. Use these as templates and inspiration when creating training sessions.
 
---
 
## SWIMMING WORKOUTS
 
### Basic Swim Workout Structure
All swim workouts typically follow this framework:
1. **Warm-up** (200-400m): Easy swimming, technique drills
2. **Drills** (200-400m): Technique-focused work
3. **Main Set**: The primary focus of the workout
4. **Cool-down** (200-400m): Easy swimming, technique work
### Common Swim Set Types
 
#### 1. **Ladder Sets**
Progressive increase OR decrease in distance.
 
**Ascending Ladder (building endurance):**
- 100, 200, 300, 400, 500m with 5-10 sec rest
- Focus: Hold consistent pace as distance increases
- Total: 1,500m
**Descending Ladder (finishing strong):**
- 500, 400, 300, 200, 100m with 5-10 sec rest
- Focus: Negative split - get faster as sets get shorter
- Total: 1,500m
**Mini Ladder (sprint/middle-distance):**
- 100, 75, 50, 25m (repeat 2-3x)
- Rest: 10-15 sec between reps
- Total: 500-750m
#### 2. **Pyramid Sets**
Increase then decrease in distance (or intensity).
 
**Distance Pyramid:**
- 50, 100, 150, 200, 150, 100, 50m
- Rest: 15 sec between reps
- Focus: Maintain same pace throughout
- Total: 800m
**Intensity Pyramid (with constant distance):**
- 5 x 100m: Easy, Moderate, Hard, Moderate, Easy
- Rest: 20 sec between reps
- Focus: Controlled build and recovery
**Full Pyramid:**
- 1x50, 2x100, 2x150, 2x200, 2x150, 2x100, 1x50
- Rest: 10-15 sec between reps
- Total: 1,400m
#### 3. **Interval Sets (Time-Based)**
Fixed send-off times regardless of finish time.
 
**10 x 100m on 2:00 (Threshold pace):**
- If you finish in 1:45, you get 15 sec rest
- If you finish in 1:50, you get 10 sec rest
- Focus: Consistent pacing
**Descending Intervals:**
- 5 x 100m on: 2:10, 2:05, 2:00, 1:55, 1:50
- Focus: Progressively faster while maintaining technique
#### 4. **Critical Swim Speed (CSS) Sets**
Sustained threshold pace work.
 
**CSS 100s Build:**
- Week 1: 3 x 100 at CSS pace
- Week 2: 4 x 100 at CSS pace
- Progress to: 10 x 100 at CSS pace
- Rest: Built into the interval (e.g., on 1:20 per 100)
**CSS Distance Scaling:**
- 3 x 500 at CSS pace (if CSS = 1:20/100, target 6:40 per 500)
- Rest: 30-45 sec between reps
#### 5. **Fartlek Swimming (Speed Play)**
Alternating speeds within continuous swim.
 
**Distance Fartlek:**
- 25 easy, 25 fast, 50 easy, 50 fast, 100 easy, 100 fast, 200 easy, 200 fast
- Then back down: 100 easy, 100 fast, 50 easy, 50 fast, 25 easy, 25 fast
- Total: 1,000m continuous
**Stroke Rate Fartlek:**
- 200m continuous alternating 25m fast turnover / 25m controlled
- Focus: Technique maintenance at varied speeds
#### 6. **Endurance Sets**
Longer continuous or semi-continuous swimming.
 
**Straight Set:**
- 4 x 400m at moderate aerobic pace
- Rest: 20-30 sec
- Focus: Even pacing
**Broken 1500:**
- 100, 200, 300, 400, 500 with 5 sec rest
- OR: 500, 400, 300, 200, 100 with 5 sec rest
- Focus: Simulate race distance with brief recoveries
#### 7. **Sprint/VO2 Max Sets**
Short, high-intensity intervals.
 
**50m Sprints:**
- 10 x 50m at maximum effort
- Rest: 20-30 sec (or on 1:00-1:15)
- Focus: Power and speed
**25m Build Sprints:**
- 20 x 25m progressively faster (each one slightly faster than previous)
- Rest: 15 sec
- Total: 500m
#### 8. **Mixed Distance Sets**
Variety to simulate race demands.
 
**Threshold + Sprint:**
- 4 x 200m at threshold pace, rest 20 sec
- 4 x 25m kick, rest 15 sec
- 8 x 50m sprint, rest 15 sec
- Total: 1,200m
**Example Complete Swim Workout (2,100m)**
- Warm-up: 200m easy
- Drills: 200m (4 x 50 different drills)
- Main Set 1: 4-3-2-1 ladder (400, 300, 200, 100) descending effort
  - Rest: matching seconds (40, 30, 20, 10)
- Main Set 2: 5 x 100 on 2:00 threshold
- Cool-down: 200m easy

## CYCLING WORKOUTS
 
### Basic Ride Structure
1. **Warm-up** (10-15 min): Progressive build from easy to moderate
2. **Main Set**: Intervals or sustained efforts
3. **Cool-down** (5-10 min): Easy spinning
### Common Bike Set Types
 
#### 1. **Sweet Spot / Tempo Intervals**
Sustained efforts at 85-95% FTP.
 
**Classic Sweet Spot:**
- 15 min warm-up
- 3 x 10 min at 88-93% FTP
- 5 min recovery between intervals
- 10 min cool-down
- Total: 60 min
**Progressive Sweet Spot:**
- 15 min warm-up
- 2 x 15 min at 85-90% FTP
- 5 min recovery
- 10 min cool-down
- Total: 70 min
#### 2. **Threshold (FTP) Intervals**
At or near functional threshold power.
 
**2x20 FTP:**
- 15 min warm-up
- 2 x 20 min at 95-100% FTP
- 5-10 min recovery between
- 10 min cool-down
- Total: 70-75 min
**3x8 FTP:**
- 15 min warm-up
- 3 x 8 min at 95-105% FTP
- 4 min recovery between intervals
- 10 min cool-down
- Total: 55 min
**5x5 FTP:**
- 12 min warm-up
- 5 x 5 min at 95-105% FTP
- 3 min recovery between intervals
- 8 min cool-down
- Total: 60 min
#### 3. **VO2 Max Intervals**
Short, high-intensity efforts above threshold.
 
**5 x 3 min VO2:**
- 15 min warm-up
- 5 x 3 min at 110-120% FTP
- 3 min recovery between
- 10 min cool-down
- Total: 55 min
**8 x 2 min VO2:**
- 12 min warm-up
- 8 x 2 min at 115-125% FTP
- 2 min recovery between
- 8 min cool-down
- Total: 52 min
#### 4. **Hill Repeats**
Power-focused climbing intervals.
 
**Short Hill Climbs:**
- 10 min warm-up
- 6 x 3 min hill at 120% FTP, 70 RPM (over-geared)
- 3 min recovery spin down/flat
- 10 min cool-down
- Total: 46 min
**Long Hill Climbs:**
- 15 min warm-up
- 3 x 8 min sustained climb at 95-105% FTP
- 5 min recovery
- 10 min cool-down
- Total: 64 min
#### 5. **Over-Under Intervals**
Alternating slightly below and above threshold.
 
**Classic Over-Unders:**
- 15 min warm-up
- 3 x 8 min: alternate 2 min @ 95% FTP, 1 min @ 105% FTP
- 4 min recovery between sets
- 10 min cool-down
- Total: 57 min
#### 6. **Pyramid Power Intervals**
 
**Power Pyramid:**
- 15 min warm-up
- 1-2-3-4-3-2-1 min at 110-120% FTP
- Equal recovery after each
- 10 min cool-down
- Total: 58 min
#### 7. **Endurance / Aerobic Rides**
Long, steady efforts in Zones 1-2.
 
**Foundation Ride:**
- 10 min Z1 warm-up
- 1-3 hours Z2 (70-80% FTP), 85-95 RPM
- 10 min Z1 cool-down
- Focus: Aerobic base, efficiency
**Progressive Endurance:**
- First half: Z1-Z2
- Last 20-30 min: Build to tempo/sweet spot
- Focus: Finishing strong
#### 8. **Fartlek / Variable Pace**
 
**Fartlek Ride:**
- 15 min warm-up
- 40 min: alternate 3 min hard (VO2) / 2 min easy
- 10 min cool-down
- Total: 65 min
**Example Complete Bike Workout (60 min)**
- Warm-up: 12 min progressive through the Easy/Warmup band
- Main Set: 6 x 3 min at Threshold
  - Recovery: 2 min in the Easy band
- Cool-down: 8 min in the Easy band
Take the actual watt ranges from `workout-zones.md`.

## RUNNING WORKOUTS
 
### Basic Run Structure
1. **Warm-up** (10-15 min): Easy jog, dynamic drills
2. **Main Set**: Intervals or tempo work
3. **Cool-down** (5-10 min): Easy jog
### Common Run Set Types
 
#### 1. **Tempo Runs**
Sustained efforts at lactate threshold.
 
**Classic Tempo:**
- 10 min easy warm-up
- 20-30 min at tempo pace (Zone 3)
- 10 min easy cool-down
- Total: 40-50 min
**Progressive Tempo:**
- 10 min easy warm-up
- 5 min moderate
- 10 min tempo
- 10 min threshold
- 10 min easy cool-down
- Total: 45 min
#### 2. **Threshold Intervals**
Intervals at or near lactate threshold.
 
**3 x 8 min Threshold:**
- 10 min easy warm-up
- 3 x 8 min at threshold pace
- 2-3 min recovery jog
- 10 min cool-down
- Total: 44-47 min
**5 x 5 min Threshold:**
- 10 min easy warm-up
- 5 x 5 min at threshold pace
- 2 min recovery jog
- 10 min cool-down
- Total: 45 min
#### 3. **VO2 Max Intervals**
Short, high-intensity repeats.
 
**Track 1000s:**
- 15 min warm-up + 4 x 20 sec strides
- 5 x 1000m at VO2 pace
- 3 min recovery jog
- 10 min cool-down
**800m Repeats:**
- 15 min warm-up + strides
- 6-8 x 800m at VO2 pace
- 2-3 min recovery
- 10 min cool-down
**400m Repeats:**
- 15 min warm-up + strides
- 10-12 x 400m at VO2 pace
- 90 sec - 2 min recovery
- 10 min cool-down
#### 4. **Fartlek (Speed Play)**
Unstructured or structured speed variations.
 
**Classic Fartlek:**
- 15 min easy warm-up
- 6 x 3 min at VO2 pace
- 2 min active recovery
- 10 min cool-down
- Total: 55 min
**Variable Fartlek:**
- 10 min easy warm-up
- 30 sec fast, 90 sec easy (repeat 10x)
- 1 min fast, 2 min easy (repeat 5x)
- 2 min fast, 3 min easy (repeat 3x)
- 10 min cool-down
#### 5. **Hill Repeats**
Strength and power development.
 
**Short Hill Sprints:**
- 15 min easy warm-up
- 8-10 x 30-45 sec uphill sprint
- Jog down recovery
- 10 min cool-down
**Long Hill Repeats:**
- 15 min warm-up
- 6 x 3 min sustained hill effort
- Jog down recovery
- 10 min cool-down
#### 6. **Pyramid Intervals**
 
**Time Pyramid:**
- 15 min warm-up + strides
- 1-2-3-4-3-2-1 min at threshold to VO2 pace
- Equal time recovery after each
- 10 min cool-down
**Distance Pyramid:**
- 15 min warm-up
- 400-800-1200-1600-1200-800-400m
- 2-3 min recovery between
- 10 min cool-down
#### 7. **Long Runs**
Aerobic endurance building.
 
**Steady Long Run:**
- 60-120+ min at conversational pace (Z1-Z2)
- Focus: Time on feet, aerobic development
**Progressive Long Run:**
- Start: Marathon pace +20%
- Finish: Marathon pace +10%
- Example: 90 min building from easy to moderate
**Long Run with Surge:**
- 75 min easy
- Last 15-20 min at marathon to tempo pace
- Total: 90-95 min
**Long Run with Fast Finish (progressive blocks):**
Run the bulk of the session at long run steady, then finish with a short
progression rather than one continuous surge.

- Structure: 2-4 consecutive blocks, each one shorter and a step quicker than
  the one before, run back-to-back with no recovery between them.
- Anchor: the blocks step down towards current goal race pace. The first block
  sits just quicker than long run steady; the last block sits just slower than
  threshold, so the run stays aerobic.
- Sizing: the fast finish is roughly the last 20-25% of the run. Longest block
  first (say 8-10 min), shortest last (say 4-5 min).
- Always follow the last block with an easy cooldown.
- Progression over weeks: lengthen the blocks first, then shift them quicker as
  the threshold band itself moves. Do not jump straight to goal race pace.

Take the actual pace bands from `workout-zones.md`, and re-anchor them whenever
the threshold band or the goal race pace changes.
#### 8. **Strides / Speed**
Short accelerations for neuromuscular development.
 
**Strides:**
- 4-8 x 20-30 sec at 5K pace or faster
- Full recovery walk/jog back
- Can be added to end of easy runs
**Example Complete Run Workout (40 min)**
- Warm-up: 10 min easy jog
- Drills: 4 x 20 sec strides with walk back recovery
- Main Set: 4 x 4 min at threshold pace
  - Recovery: 90 sec jog
- Cool-down: 10 min easy jog

## BRICK WORKOUTS
Combining disciplines back-to-back to practice transitions.
 
### Short Course Brick
- Bike: 45-60 min with last 10 min at race intensity
- Transition: Quick change
- Run: 4 x 3 min at race pace, 2 min recovery
- Cool-down: 5 min easy
### Long Course Brick
- Bike: 2-3 hours aerobic with last 20 min at race pace
- Transition: Practice nutrition, change
- Run: 20-30 min at marathon pace
- Cool-down: 10 min easy

## WORKOUT PLANNING PRINCIPLES
 
### Progressive Overload
- Increase volume or intensity by ~10% per week
- Every 3-4 weeks include a recovery week (reduce by 20-30%)
### Periodization
- **Base Phase:** High volume, low intensity (70-80% Z1-Z2)
- **Build Phase:** Moderate volume, mixed intensity (60-70% Z1-Z2, 30-40% Z3-Z5)
- **Peak Phase:** Lower volume, race-specific intensity
- **Taper:** Significant volume reduction, maintain intensity
### Workout Distribution (80/20 Rule)
- 80% of training at low intensity (Z1-Z2)
- 20% at moderate to high intensity (Z3-Z5)
### Recovery Guidelines
- Hard workout → Easy day (or rest)
- High-intensity sessions need 48-72 hours between similar efforts
- Listen to your body - adjust based on fatigue, soreness, motivation
---
 
## NOTES ON WORKOUT CREATION
 
1. **Always include warm-up and cool-down** - prevents injury and aids recovery
2. **Set specific zones/paces** - vague targets lead to poor execution
3. **Match workout to training phase** - base vs. build vs. peak
4. **Consider weekly load** - don't stack multiple hard days
5. **Use variety** - mix interval types to prevent staleness
6. **Progressive difficulty** - start conservative, build over weeks
7. **Recovery is training** - easy days should be truly easy
8. **Specificity matters** - race-specific intensity as event approaches

This library provides foundational workout structures. Adapt distances, intervals, and intensities based on athlete fitness level, training phase, and race goals.
